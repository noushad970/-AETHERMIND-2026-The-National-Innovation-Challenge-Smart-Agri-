# AI Agriculture Assistant for Bangladesh

## Project Overview

**AI Agriculture Assistant** is a modern AI-powered mobile application designed to help farmers manage their crops from planting to harvesting. The application serves as a complete digital farming companion by combining artificial intelligence, weather forecasting, crop management, and agricultural guidance into a single platform. It aims to improve productivity, reduce crop losses, and provide real-time support for farmers, especially in Bangladesh.

The application will be developed using **Flutter** for cross-platform mobile development and **Firebase** as the backend service for authentication, cloud storage, notifications, and real-time data management. Initially, users will be able to register and log in using their email addresses. In future versions, **BD Apps phone number verification and subscription services** will be integrated to provide premium AI features and additional agricultural services.

---

# Project Objectives

The primary objective of the application is to provide farmers with an intelligent assistant that guides them through every stage of crop cultivation. Instead of relying on multiple sources for weather, disease diagnosis, fertilizer schedules, and farming advice, farmers can access everything from a single mobile application.

The application is designed to:

- Help farmers identify crop diseases using AI image recognition.
- Provide farming advice in simple Bangla through an AI chatbot.
- Track crop growth from planting to harvest.
- Generate automatic farming schedules.
- Send reminders for important farming activities.
- Provide weather-based recommendations.
- Display daily market prices of agricultural products.
- Maintain farm records and estimate harvest yields.

# Crop Growth Tracker (Core Feature)

The Crop Growth Tracker is the main feature of the application.

When a farmer plants a crop, they first select the crop type (for example, Rice, Wheat, Tomato, Potato, Mango, or Banana). The farmer then enters information such as:

- Planting date
- Cultivated land area
- Number of trees (for orchards)
- Crop variety (optional)

The application automatically generates a complete cultivation timeline based on agricultural best practices.

### Example (Rice)

If a rice variety requires approximately **90 days** to mature, the application creates a complete farming calendar such as:

- Day 1 – Prepare the land
- Day 2 – Plant seedlings
- Day 7 – First irrigation
- Day 15 – Weed removal
- Day 20 – Apply first fertilizer
- Day 35 – Pest inspection
- Day 45 – Apply second fertilizer
- Day 60 – Disease monitoring
- Day 80 – Check crop color and maturity
- Day 90 – Harvest

Each activity includes a live countdown timer and automatic reminders, allowing farmers to complete tasks on time.

The application also tracks:

- Days remaining until harvest
- Current crop growth stage
- Completed farming activities
- Upcoming tasks
- Estimated harvest date
- Crop health status

For tree-based farming such as mango or banana, farmers can record the total number of trees, add newly planted trees, mark dead trees, and monitor the growth status of individual trees.

# AI Features

The application integrates Artificial Intelligence to assist farmers with everyday agricultural problems.

### AI Crop Disease Detection

Farmers can capture or upload a photo of a crop leaf or fruit. The AI analyzes the image and identifies potential diseases, nutrient deficiencies, or pest attacks. It then provides:

- Disease name
- Confidence score
- Possible causes
- Prevention methods
- Recommended treatment
- Organic and chemical solutions

### AI Agricultural Chatbot

Farmers can ask questions in Bangla using text or voice.

Example questions include:

- Why are my rice leaves turning yellow?
- Which fertilizer should I apply today?
- How can I control insects in tomato plants?
- Is today a good day for irrigation?

The AI responds with simple, practical, and easy-to-understand guidance.
